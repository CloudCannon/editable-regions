{{- /* Builds the single live-editing bundle: the runtime entry asset, rendered with the site's snapshot (template sources, site config) via resources.ExecuteAsTemplate, bundled by js.Build and fingerprinted for cache-busting. Templates from the site's themes and vendored modules are captured on top (walk-modules.html) and merged under the project's files, so a component including a theme/vendored partial resolves in the editor.
